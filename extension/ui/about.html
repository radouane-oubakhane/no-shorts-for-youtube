No Shorts for YouTube

Independent browser extension

How it works

Recognized Shorts cards and shelves are hidden. Optional controls remove navigation and redirect Shorts-specific URLs. Regular video titles and durations are not used to filter content.

Privacy

The extension processes Shorts-related page elements and URLs locally in your browser. It stores only its three boolean preferences in Chrome's local extension storage. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal information, browsing history, video titles or account data. It has no analytics, advertising, external server or remote code.

Scope and limitations

Only supported YouTube website hosts are affected. A Short exposed as a normal video without a Shorts marker may remain visible. YouTube layout changes may require an extension update. This extension is not affiliated with YouTube or Google.

Control your data

Reset preferences using the popup, or uninstall the extension to remove its stored settings.
