# No Shorts for YouTube — Privacy policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026.

No Shorts is an independent Chrome extension for hiding and optionally blocking navigation to YouTube Shorts.

The extension examines Shorts-related page markup and URLs locally in the browser to identify content and navigation. It does not transmit or retain browsing history, video titles, personal information, account data, or page content. The only information persisted by the extension is three boolean preferences (master protection, navigation hiding, and direct-link blocking), saved in Chrome's local extension storage. There is no analytics, advertising, remotely hosted code, or external server.

The extension does not sell, share or transfer user data. Preferences may be reset from the popup or removed by uninstalling the extension. YouTube's own processing is subject to its own privacy policy. The extension is not affiliated with YouTube or Google.
